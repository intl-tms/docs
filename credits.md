# Credits

## next-intl

Intl TMS stands on [next-intl](https://next-intl.dev), the internationalization
library for Next.js created and maintained by
[Jan Amann](https://github.com/amannn).

Intl TMS is not an i18n library. In every guide, in the demo app and in the
Storybook examples, next-intl does all the real work: it loads the messages,
picks the locale, formats plurals and dates, and routes between languages.
Intl TMS only adds a way to edit the messages next-intl renders.

- The [getting started guide](/developers/getting-started) follows the shape
  of next-intl's
  [App Router setup](https://next-intl.dev/docs/getting-started/app-router).
- The [demo app](https://github.com/intl-tms/demo-app) is a next-intl website
  with Intl TMS added on top.

Intl TMS is not affiliated with or endorsed by next-intl.

If next-intl helps you too, you can
[star it on GitHub](https://github.com/amannn/next-intl) or
[sponsor its development](https://github.com/sponsors/amannn).
